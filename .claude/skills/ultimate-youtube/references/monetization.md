# Monetization

Thresholds change and differ by country. Always tell the user to confirm on
YouTube's help pages (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851) and in
YouTube Studio's Earn tab, which shows their own eligibility.

## YouTube Partner Program (as last published; verify)

- **Fan funding tier** (memberships, Super Thanks/Chat/Stickers, Shopping in
  available countries): about 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in the last 90
  days, and either 3,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 3M public Shorts
  views in 90 days.
- **Full tier** (adds ad revenue, including the Shorts revenue pool): about
  1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10M
  public Shorts views in 90 days.
- Requirements also include following monetization policies, living in an
  eligible country, 2-step verification and an AdSense account.
- Reused or repetitive content (compilations, unedited reuploads, mass-produced
  templated videos with little original value) can be refused under the
  "inauthentic content" policy. Original commentary, narration, editing,
  animation and storytelling add value.

## Revenue streams

| Stream | Fits when | First step |
|---|---|---|
| Ads (YPP) | eligible; advertiser-friendly topics | eligibility; avoid unnecessary demonetizing content |
| Channel memberships | loyal community | 2-3 simple perks (early access, extras, badges) |
| Super Thanks / Chat / Stickers | engaged audience, lives/premieres | mention once, never pressure |
| Sponsorships | clear audience demographic | media kit: audience, views per video, engagement |
| Affiliate | products naturally used in videos | links in description with disclosure |
| Own products | trust + repeat requests | digital products from your expertise |
| Services/consulting | expertise niches | link to a page in each relevant video |
| Licensing / stock | original footage, art, animation | license distinctive assets |
| Shopping | product-focused niches, eligible country | tag relevant products |

## Revenue math

RPM (revenue per 1,000 views, after YouTube's share) is what the creator earns;
CPM is what advertisers pay. Both vary widely by niche, audience country, season
(Q4 highest) and format (Shorts RPM is typically much lower than long-form). Use
the channel's own `yt_analytics.py revenue` numbers; don't quote generic RPMs as fact.

## Disclosure

Paid promotions must be declared in YouTube Studio ("includes paid promotion")
and follow local advertising law.
