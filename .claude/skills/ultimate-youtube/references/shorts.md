# Shorts

## Mechanics

- Vertical or square video up to 3 minutes (YouTube raised the limit from 60 s in
  October 2024). Shorts over 1 minute with a Content ID claim may be blocked.
- Shown in the Shorts feed, where viewers swipe; the key signals are whether
  they keep watching, replay, engage, or swipe away.
- Shorts views count when playback starts (since March 2025), so view counts are
  not comparable with long-form and older Shorts. Compare Shorts with Shorts only.
- Monetized through the Shorts revenue-sharing pool (YPP).
- Shorts can link to a related long video (set in the Shorts editor).

## What works

- The first second decides: visual motion + words that create a question.
- One idea per Short. Payoff before the end. Loops (ending flows into the start)
  can raise replays.
- Native on-screen text sized for phones; captions for sound-off viewing.
- Cut-downs from long videos work when the moment stands alone with its own hook.
- Series formats ("Part 1/2", recurring segment) build returning viewers.

## Strategy by goal

| Goal | Shorts role |
|---|---|
| Fast discovery for a new channel | test many topics cheaply; promote winners to long-form |
| Grow a long-form channel | teasers/cut-downs linking to the long video; same audience and topics |
| Shorts-first channel | high volume, consistent format, strong series identity |

Watch whether Shorts viewers also watch long-form (`yt_analytics.py audience` ->
`creator_content_type`; subscriber growth vs long-form views). If they're a
separate audience, keep Shorts topics close to long-form topics, or accept
Shorts as their own product.
