# Strategy: positioning, growth plan, monetization

Use after an audit or onboarding, or when the user asks "what should my channel
be / how do I grow / how do I make money".

## Positioning

From the profile, scan and competitor work, write:
- **Who it's for** (one specific viewer) and **the promise** (what they get that
  they can't get elsewhere).
- **Content pillars**: 3-5 recurring themes or series, each with evidence of demand.
- **Formats**: long-form/Shorts/live mix by tier and goal.
- **Differentiation**: what the creator does that competitors don't (angle,
  depth, language, production style, personality, access). Use the competitor gap map.

## Growth plan (30/60/90 days)

For each phase: focus, output (uploads per week, Shorts), experiments (one
variable at a time), and a target metric with the source script that measures it.

Tier guidance:
- **New (<1K)**: find a repeatable format; search-led topics and Shorts for
  discovery; packaging practice; 1-2 uploads/week sustained.
- **Growing (1K-10K)**: double down on outlier topics; series and playlists;
  improve retention of the first 30 seconds; start a watchlist.
- **Established (10K-100K)**: systematise (calendar, templates), expand formats
  or languages carefully, diversify revenue.
- **Authority (100K+)**: protect what works, test new formats on a side series,
  partnerships, owned products.

## Monetization

Read `references/monetization.md`. Assess which streams fit the audience and
niche (ads, memberships, Super Thanks, sponsorships, affiliate, products,
services, licensing) and the current eligibility. Use `yt_comments.py` monetization
signals and `yt_analytics.py revenue` if available. Recommend the next one or
two streams with concrete first steps; don't list everything.

## Output

`templates/strategy-plan.md`. Every recommendation needs evidence or a
labelled assumption, and a metric to judge it.
